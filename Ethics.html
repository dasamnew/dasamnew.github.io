Guidelines for Developers

"When you develop software, you must prioritize user privacy and data security. This means ensuring data confidentiality and complying with regulations like GDPR to protect personal information."

"Effective communication with stakeholders is essential in software development, as it sets the foundation for transparency and honesty throughout the project. Upholding these ethical principles strengthens relationships and promotes accountability."

Source: https://www.bettersoftware.dev/blog/software-development-ethics/

"Contribute to society and to human well-being, acknowledging that all people are stakeholders in computing.This principle, which concerns the quality of life of all people, affirms an obligation of computing professionals, both individually and collectively, to use their skills for the benefit of society, its members, and the environment surrounding them."

"Be fair and take action not to discriminate. The values of equality, tolerance, respect for others, and justice govern this principle. Fairness requires that even careful decision processes provide some avenue for redress of grievances."

Source: https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics

"Software development is not merely a technical endeavor; it is also a deeply ethical one. Every line of code written, every feature implemented, and every decision made by developers can have profound implications for individuals, communities, and society as a whole."

Source: https://medium.com/@akash.chhabra/ethical-considerations-in-software-development-a-critical-discussion-for-and-beyond-2a1eb1dcaf06
